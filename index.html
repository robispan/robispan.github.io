HeartSnap — iOS App

The Heart Rate app measures your heart rate using your iPhone's camera and flashlight. Simply place your finger over the camera lens and watch as the app accurately detects your pulse through subtle color changes in your fingertip.

Features a clean, minimalist interface that displays your real-time heart rate with precision. All measurements are automatically stored in your personal history, and everything stays private on your device. No additional hardware required.

Status: In development — preparing for App Store release.
