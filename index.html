HeartSnap

An iOS app that measures your heart rate with the phone’s camera. Put a fingertip over the camera and flash, and hold still. Each heartbeat changes how much light passes through your skin, and HeartSnap reads it for fifteen seconds.

Readings stay on your device and in your own private iCloud. No account, no sign-up.
