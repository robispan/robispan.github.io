HeartSnap Plus subscriptions

HeartSnap is free to use and lets you take up to three readings each day. HeartSnap Plus is an optional auto-renewing subscription that removes the daily limit and unlocks your full reading history and the Insights screens.

  • Plans and length. HeartSnap Plus is offered as a monthly subscription (one month per period) and an annual subscription (one year per period). Both give the same access for the whole of each period.
  • Price. The price is shown in the App Store and in the app, in your local currency, before you confirm anything. In the United States it is $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year; prices in other regions are set by Apple's equivalent price tiers and may differ.
  • Payment. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account when you confirm the purchase. HeartSnap never receives or stores your payment details.
  • Automatic renewal. The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends, at the price of the plan you are on.
  • Managing and cancelling. You can manage the subscription or turn off auto-renew at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
  • Price changes. If the price of your plan changes, Apple will notify you in advance, and where Apple requires it your subscription will not renew at the higher price unless you agree to it.
  • Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple rather than from us, through reportaproblem.apple.com.

If a subscription lapses or is cancelled, your readings are not deleted. The free daily limit applies again, and your full history becomes visible once more if you resubscribe.
